# Third-party components and sources

This MVP uses FastAPI/Starlette, Uvicorn, Pydantic, HTTPX, Beautiful Soup, NumPy, OpenCV, yt-dlp, and imageio-ffmpeg; pytest and Playwright are development tools. Their distributions retain their respective upstream licenses and notices. `requirements.lock` records installed Python package versions. FFmpeg and Chromium are separately supplied binaries with their own license terms.

Match data and source links come from The Blue Alliance. Downloaded recordings retain the original creators' rights; no ownership or redistribution license is asserted. The downloaded frc9550 YOLO26n model (Team 9550, distinct from Team 955) is declared Apache-2.0 by its publisher; its original model card and license are retained in data/models/frc9550. Ultralytics uses AGPL-3.0 (or its commercial license), and PyTorch/torchvision have their own licenses. No team training dataset is bundled. See the research documents for upstream project links and license findings.

Automatic robot detection now uses the pinned public RobotTracking robot_detector3 checkpoint (ai4funhobby-oss/RobotTracking, revision b1af3ef2811f59c25e3908cc96720707e055e66e). No repository license was found during the earlier research pass; the downloaded artifact is kept in the ignored local data directory for this evaluation, and no redistribution license is asserted. The earlier Team 955 checkpoint remains a local comparison artifact. RapidOCR/PP-OCRv4 supplies OCR, ONNX Runtime executes it on CPU, and SciPy supplies tracking assignment. Preserve their upstream notices.
